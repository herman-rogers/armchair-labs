How to read these rankings

Overall rank orders the positions included in this campaign by the model’s directly predicted fantasy-point total. Position rank uses the same points within each position. This is a points leaderboard, not a draft-value or value-over-replacement ranking; QB scoring naturally puts many quarterbacks near the top. Available positions are shown in the filters; kickers and defenses are outside this campaign.

RB, WR, and TE use PPR scoring: 1 point per reception, 0.1 per rushing or receiving yard, and 6 per touchdown. QB uses 0.04 per passing yard, 4 per passing touchdown, −2 per interception, 0.1 per rushing yard, and 6 per rushing touchdown. Fumbles and bonuses are excluded; QB receiving and RB/WR/TE passing are excluded.

Actual ranks use the saved nflverse standard PPR totals, including its fumble and conversion scoring, without league-specific bonuses. This differs from the narrower model scoring contract. Actual overall ranks cover the positions in this campaign, including observed players outside the model pool. Ties share a rank (1, 2, 2, 4). A dash means no recorded result in this snapshot, not zero points. Actual ranks remain season-to-date when switching forecast views.

Points are predicted by a separate target model, so they can differ from points calculated using the displayed component statistics. Points per game divide by scheduled team games, including games a player might miss; these are flat averages, not matchup-specific forecasts. All saved candidates are included, including players with little projected production. Teams reflect the saved snapshot.

These forecasts reconstruct an earlier cutoff from later source captures. Models trained through 2025; these are saved research predictions, not live injury-adjusted rankings.
