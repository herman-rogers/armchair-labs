# Research-path coverage after the follow-up experiments

Every recommendation from the [source review](../../docs/research/nfl_player_research_literature_review_2026-09-23.md)
and original lab queue now has an executed test or a documented data boundary, with the
feasible narrower test completed. This closes the declared experiment menu; it does not
claim exhaustive science or label missing-data questions solved. All results are exploratory.

Start with the [readout](followup_readout.md) or [interactive report](runs/review_003/index.html).
The [Protocol 2 menu](followup_protocol.md) and [subsequent closure checks](closure_protocol.md)
distinguish work declared before results from later diagnostic extensions.

| Research path | Executed work and evidence | Disposition / requirement to reopen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample-aware strengths | One/three-season raw and empirical-Bayes rates; role priors; published-count NGS pooling; future efficiency and point targets. [Signal summaries](runs/signals_001/signals_summaries.json) | Tested, no corrected general increment. Useful descriptive rates remain possible. RYOE eligible-count reconciliation and expectation-model vintages remain incomplete. |
| QB opening role, duration and retention | Actual schedule starting-QB labels; designed/scramble and prior-start features; dated news; opening mixture; non-overlapping weekly role blocks. [Weekly results](runs/structure_001/weekly_roles_summaries.json) | Weekly role prediction is promising. Preseason mixture/news adds no convincing total-output gain. Complete dated news coverage and evaluation of previously unseen backups would extend the task. |
| Receiving opportunity and execution | Initial participation/target-earning proxy trials; catch fraction, YAC per reception, air yards per target, NGS; WR/TE efficiency and workload targets. [Signals](runs/signals_001/signals_summaries.json) | Tested; no reliable incremental point-model gain. All-player route counts, alignment/openness and compatible live source clocks remain missing. Passing-play participation is not routes run. |

Keep an independent uncertainty candidate; freeze before prospective validation. |
| Joint opportunity/execution | WR/TE appearance hurdle, positive workload/rate, product of means, independent/paired earlier-OOF residual simulations. [Point results](runs/distributions_001/joint_point_summaries.json), [distribution results](runs/distributions_001/joint_score_summaries.json) | CRPS improves against direct residual simulation; little point-mean gain and conservative intervals. Dependence coupling itself has no convincing advantage over independence. No coherent all-team joint distribution is established. |
| Team volume and allocation | Forecast team attempts/carries; residual share from earlier cutoff cohorts; full/half player reconciliation; missing-team and traded-player flags. [Results](runs/structure_001/team_allocation_summaries.json) | Partial RB adjustment is promising; full reconciliation harms MSE. Next candidate needs explicit uncertainty in team budgets and future trades, not oracle totals. |
| College development and translation | Pre-draft-style versus post-draft selected-rookie tests; prior college histories, shares, trends, peaks, transfers; identity/exit/biography audit. [Rookie results](runs/structure_001/rookie_summaries.json), [data audit](runs/audit_002/data_path_audit.json) | No development increment beyond draft capital. Full NFL-entry/exit-cohort work remains data-limited: unmatched identity is not a negative outcome. Verified exits, competition and historical biography vintages are required for broader claims. |
| Availability | Snap versus scoring appearance ascertainment; complete-game source checks; prior injury-report descriptors versus history/market; retain missing identities. [Results](runs/structure_001/availability_summaries.json) | No corrected MSE gain. This predicts offensive participation, not medical injury. Active/inactive and injury-caused-absence labels would be needed for those distinct outcomes. Local reports include 2025. |
| Age, retention and era | With/without age for ≥8 scoring appearances and conditional execution; five-year versus expanding training; age slices. [Results](runs/audit_002/age_recency_summaries.json) | No corrected increment from these variants. Do not interpret this as an identified biological aging curve; selection and other age-correlated features remain. |
| Draft/waiver/trade utility | Real league/history audit; original global and conditional-quantile fixed-slot selection; median/lower bounds; explicit zero-quantile tie sensitivity. [Diagnostics](runs/review_003/supplemental_diagnostics.json) | No demonstrated decision value. Current 2026 transactions exist, but completed historical decision-time rosters, feasible alternatives, prices, scoring and constraints are absent for the evaluation history. No profit or policy-validation claim. |
| Context-adjusted attribution | Same-team peer/relative efficiency ablations; stronger 224-feature archive control; provider vintage and denominator audit. [Signals](runs/signals_001/signals_summaries.json), [audit](runs/audit_002/data_path_audit.json) | Observational forecast test completed. Causal ability, protection/pressure attribution and alternative QB target decisions remain unidentified. New multilevel/play-level attribution or fold-fitted xFP would require a separate estimand/protocol; the tested residual features do not reproduce nflWAR or EHCP. |
| Tracking scouting / non-skill positions | Schema inventory of 217 pinned NFL provider tables; primary-route/pressure field checks. [Audit](runs/audit_002/data_path_audit.json) | Data-limited: zero trajectory tables. Route clustering, STRAIN and NFL Ghosts require actual player trajectories, identity/label coverage and position-specific validation. Summary NGS is not a substitute. |
| Validation and support | Corrected archive matching, stage-wise Holm families, population/era slices, exposure-weighted versus player-weighted efficiency, low-prior-volume/high-market/team-change/position-change slices, career/franchise/two-season block sensitivity, chronology/write tests. [Dependence](runs/audit_002/dependence_sensitivity.json), [support](runs/audit_002/support_slices.json), [exposure](runs/audit_002/exposure_weighted_efficiency.json) | Executed. Team-change support uses recorded abbreviations and is labeled accordingly; the allocation model normalizes franchise aliases. No method solves all dependence. Freeze 2027 preseason predictions before games for new prospective evidence; capture is specified, not scheduled. |

The preferred architecture remains **separate candidate components**: uncertainty, weekly QB
role, and partial RB allocation. Bulk signal combinations and standalone player scores have
not earned adoption. A combined next-generation model would need its own fixed comparison;
it is not justified merely by selecting favorable rows across these experiments.

Data-limited work has explicit reopening requirements above. No empty model stub is counted
as a successful experiment, and no source acquisition or production integration was hidden
inside this lab work. Deleting this directory removes code, tests, reports and results.
